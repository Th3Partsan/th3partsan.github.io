How to get the plans

Every job card has a blue 'Request plans & specs' button at the bottom. Tap it and a panel opens with the request email ready to go — tap 'Open in my mail app & send' inside it, or copy the address if your mail app doesn’t open. For most jobs, the plans, specs, and step-by-step bid instructions come back right away by email, usually within the hour. For the few jobs marked as still being pulled, you’ll get the bid instructions and an acknowledgment right away, and the plans will follow by email as soon as they’re in. Where the owner posts addenda on an official portal, the reply points you there too.

“Carrier specified” means Carrier is the named equipment on the job. “Carrier Alternate” means another brand is specified and Carrier is the alternate that can be submitted.
